Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Victoria Wordsworth ("I", "me", "my") handles personal information collected through this website (victoriawordsworth.com). I am committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. What personal information I collect

Depending on how you interact with this website, I may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact details: your name and email address, if you submit the contact form.
  • Message content: the content of any message you send via the contact form.
  • IP addresses: automatically received by GitHub Pages and other third-party services embedded on this site as part of serving web content.
  • Device and browser information: browser type, operating system, and device type, collected automatically by GitHub Pages and potentially other third-party services embedded on this site.
  • Usage and referral data: pages visited, referring URLs, and time of visit, collected automatically by GitHub Pages and potentially other third-party services embedded on this site.

2. How I collect it

  • Contact form: when you voluntarily submit your name, email address, and message via the contact form on this site.
  • Automatic collection by GitHub Pages: when you visit this site, GitHub Pages automatically collects server log data including your IP address, browser type, and pages accessed.
  • Behold Instagram widget: when you view the photo feed section of this site, the Behold widget loads content from Behold's servers, which may receive your IP address.
  • Third-party CDNs: icons and animation styles are loaded from unpkg.com and Cloudflare's CDN respectively, whose servers may receive your IP address and browser information as part of serving those assets.

3. Why I collect it and how I use it

Purpose Information used
Responding to enquiries submitted via the contact form Name, email address, message content
Delivering and maintaining the website (hosting infrastructure) IP address, device/browser data, usage data (collected by GitHub Pages)
Displaying my Instagram photography feed IP address, device and browser information (may be received by Behold)
Loading icons and animation styles via CDN IP address, device and browser information (may be received by unpkg.com and Cloudflare)

I do not use your personal information for marketing, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Who I share it with

I use the following third-party service providers who may receive your personal information as part of delivering their services:

  • GitHub, Inc. (United States): this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub automatically collects server log data including IP addresses and browser information. GitHub is bound by its own Privacy Statement.
  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com, United States): contact form submissions are transmitted to and processed by Web3Forms, which forwards the submission to my email address. Web3Forms receives your name, email address, and message content. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy for details.
  • Behold (Behold Technologies, Inc.) (United States): the Instagram photo feed on this site is powered by Behold. When you view the feed, your IP address may be received by Behold's servers. See the Behold Privacy Policy for details.
  • unpkg.com (CDN, United States): icons used on this site are loaded from unpkg.com, a public CDN. Your IP address and browser information may be received by their servers as part of this request.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) (United States): animation styles used on this site are loaded from Cloudflare's CDN. Your IP address and browser information may be received by their servers as part of this request.

I do not share your personal information with any other third parties, and I do not sell your data.

5. Overseas disclosure

Personal information collected through this website is processed by service providers located in the United States (GitHub, Inc., Behold Technologies, Inc., Web3Forms, unpkg.com, and Cloudflare).

By using this website, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws may differ from those in Australia.

6. How I store and secure your information

  • Contact form submissions: forwarded to my private email inbox by Web3Forms. I retain enquiry emails for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and for record-keeping purposes, and delete them when no longer needed.
  • Server log data: retained by GitHub Pages in accordance with GitHub's data retention policies.
  • Security measures: this website is served exclusively over HTTPS. While I take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. I cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or from this site.

7. Cookies and tracking technologies

I do not set cookies of my own on this website. However, third-party services embedded in this site may set cookies or use similar tracking technologies:

  • GitHub Pages may set functional cookies necessary for serving the site.
  • Behold may set cookies when the Instagram feed widget loads.
  • CDN providers (unpkg.com and Cloudflare) may set cookies when loading icons and animation styles.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the display of the Instagram feed widget but will not otherwise affect your use of this site.

8. Accessing and correcting your information

Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), you have the right to request access to personal information I hold about you, and to request that inaccurate, out-of-date, or incomplete information be corrected. To make an access, correction, or privacy complaint, please contact me at hello@victoriawordsworth.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. I encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed of any updates.
